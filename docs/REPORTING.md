# ScamShield Reporting

## Purpose

ScamShield helps users organize information about suspected fraud and prepare reports for appropriate authorities and service providers.

## Report Types

ScamShield may help document:

- Online scams
- Social-media scams
- Cryptocurrency scams
- Investment scams
- Impersonation scams
- Phishing
- Romance scams
- Payment fraud
- Suspicious websites and accounts

## Evidence

A report may include:

- Messages and communications
- Website addresses
- Email addresses
- Phone numbers
- Social-media usernames
- Cryptocurrency wallet addresses
- Transaction identifiers
- Dates and times
- Screenshots and other supporting evidence

## Review Before Reporting

Users should review the information collected by ScamShield before submitting a report.

ScamShield should distinguish between verified facts, user-provided information, detected indicators, and analytical conclusions.

## Authority Reporting

ScamShield should direct users toward appropriate official reporting channels.

The application should not falsely claim that a report has been submitted to a government agency unless the submission has actually been completed through an authorized process.
